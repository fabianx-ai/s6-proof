# L4 - Separated global assembly and cusp shift

**Source target:** S6 Theorem 6.2 and Proposition 6.3 for assembly; Definition 6.6, Proposition 6.7,
and Section 7.5 for the cusp winding class.

**Imported interface:** The open family and three local fillings glue through the prescribed
identifications to a compact connected Hausdorff complex threefold proper over `P1`. The original
assembly is the zero cusp translation, so its winding class satisfies `ell0 = 0`.

## Prerequisites

## Required checks

- the equivalence relation is closed and the quotient is Hausdorff and second countable;
- independence of auxiliary radii, branches, and local coordinates;
- properness of the map to the compact base;
- the assembled cusp chart uses no additional translation section;
- the same winding class becomes the exponent in the later boundary relation.
